FAQs

And answers

How far in advance should we book?

Most couples reach out nine to eighteen months ahead, and the best dates go first. Once you know your date, it is worth speaking with us early, even before every detail is settled.

Do you travel?

All the time. We have photographed weddings in L.A., Miami, London, Prague, Marrakech, and Tel Aviv, and we are as at home on another continent as we are an hour from the studio. Travel is built into your collection, so wherever in the world your day is, the studio comes to it.

How does booking work?

It begins with a conversation. We talk through your day, help you find the collection that fits it, and hold your date once you are ready. From there, the studio takes over, and the planning begins long before anyone lifts a camera.

Will we meet our photographers before the day?

Yes. Your engagement session is part of every collection, and it is where you and the people photographing your wedding get to know one another, so that by the day itself, no one in front of the camera feels like a stranger.

Is an album included?

Albums are designed by our own team and can be part of your collection or added whenever you are ready. They are made to be passed down, and we guide you through every step of building one.

Do you shoot film or Super 8?

Yes. Real Super 8 and film can be added to any collection. And without it, our digital work still reaches for that look, shot on vintage lenses and graded by hand to carry the warmth and grain of film.

How long do you keep our images?

Your photographs and films are backed up to our own servers the night they are taken and kept safe well beyond your delivery, so your day is never at risk of being lost.

Can we visit your showroom?

Yes. It is where you can see the work the way it is meant to be seen, printed, bound, and on the walls. Come sit with our team to design your album or choose your wall art. Visits are by appointment.

About us

A studio in the truest sense.

It works like this. While one photographer stands above the room holding the whole scene in frame, the light, the crowd, the feeling of the night, someone is always close by to catch a father's face as he watches his daughter dance. And it goes on this way all day. The vows and the hand that reaches to wipe a tear. The toast and the table laughing at it. The dress before it is worn and the look on a mother's face when she sees it. Nothing falls to a single pair of eyes, and nothing is missed.

The timeline is built with the planner before the day begins. The celebration and the quiet moments around it are captured together, freely and unprompted, so the day can be lived rather than managed.

Our philosophy

A wedding does not live in its images. It lives in what those images bring back.

Ask most people about their wedding and they will not describe a photograph. They will describe a feeling. The hush before the doors opened. The face they searched for in the crowd. The exact moment the room tipped from ceremony into celebration.

That is the real philosophy. Not a single day, but a lifetime of them, kept in the same hands.

Our albums

An album should not live on a shelf.

The day deserves more than a book you open twice a year and put away. So we design ours to be seen. To sit on the coffee table. To rest where guests gather. To become part of the home you are building together.

Every album is designed in a clean, contemporary style that lets the photographs speak for themselves. The spreads are custom, never templated, each one composed to carry its part of the story. The exterior is yours to shape, finished to match the rooms it will live in, or kept quiet and timeless.

Master album collection

Handcrafted in Italy, made to be timeless.

Albums are handcrafted in Italy, encased in a leather-bound album showbox case. The cover, back, and spine can be customized, with a crystal glance photo cover option, a choice of case color and ribbon, and a message or monogram if you wish.

Made to be seen

A modern object, made to be displayed rather than hidden away.

Each album can rest in a showbox of its own. It can hold the album inside or stand on its own, and a few are built with a screen that plays your wedding film on a loop, so your photographs, your film, and the box itself become one piece in your home.

Wherever it sits, the intention is the same. Not a keepsake tucked away, but something on display, something that draws the eye the moment someone walks into your home.

Our albums

The coffee table book

For couples who want something grander, we make coffee table books in the spirit of a fashion or travel volume. Bound at the scale of a book you would buy for its own sake, not a wedding album but a volume that happens to be about the best day of your life.

It lives in the open, where people gather, the kind of book someone picks up while they wait for you and keeps turning. The book a guest reaches for and cannot put down.

Album design

Built with you, page by page.

  1. 01

    Select photos

    Easily select your photos via your online gallery, or opt to have our album team select them for you.

  2. 02

    Start designing

    Once selected, our team begins your first draft, and you choose your design preferences from our layout options.

  3. 03

    Touch up

    Share feedback on your first draft via our interactive album design platform, or in our studio.

  4. 04

    Finishing touches

    After the layout is approved, you finalize the album title, colors, and cover style — the home of your memories.

  5. 05

    Final approval

    To ensure perfection, we send you the final album draft to review before anything is made.

  6. 06

    Production

    Our albums are handcrafted in our lab in Italy. Upon arrival at our showroom, each one is inspected and then available for pickup or delivery.

Beyond the album

Your favorite frames are meant for the walls of the home you live in.

The image that stops you every time deserves a place you will see it daily, a frame, a wall, a room where life happens. We design fine prints, framed pieces, and wall art built to last, sized and finished to suit the space they will hang in. A portrait above the mantel, a gallery wall along the stairs, a single quiet print by the bed. Ask us when you are ready, or explore the options in your online gallery.
